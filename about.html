About GreenSync

We combine IoT technology with machine learning to make plant care effortless, smart, and sustainable.

🌱

Smart Monitoring

Real-time soil moisture, temperature, humidity, and NPK readings from IoT sensors.

🔌

ESP32 Connected

All sensor data is transmitted wirelessly through ESP32 microcontrollers.

💧

Auto Watering

Control water pumps manually from the dashboard or let the system handle it.

🧠

ML Powered

Our machine learning model predicts when your plants need water and acts automatically.

GreenSync is an IoT-powered self-watering plant system built with ESP32 microcontrollers, soil moisture sensors, temperature & humidity sensors, and NPK nutrient sensors. Our ML model analyzes real-time data to automate watering decisions, ensuring your plants always get exactly what they need.
